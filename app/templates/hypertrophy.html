Hypertrophy & muscle groups

This week's (Monday–today) working-set volume and frequency per muscle group, and whether exercises trained this week are actually progressing. Volume bands are Schoenfeld et al. 2017's dose-response findings (<5 sets/week suboptimal, 5-9 moderate, 10-20 the well-supported "optimal" range, >20 where evidence thins and recovery cost starts working against you) — a single fixed band, not tiered by experience level. Frequency reflects the finding that training a muscle group 2+ times/week outperforms 1x/week at equal volume. Neither is medical guidance, just a well-cited training-science reference point.
