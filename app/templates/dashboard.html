Weekly minutes in each %HRR training zone (Karvonen: resting + pct*(max-resting), Z1 50-60% recovery, Z2 60-70% aerobic base, Z3 70-80% aerobic/tempo, Z4 80-90% threshold, Z5 90%+ maximal). Max HR is the mean of three age-based formulas (Fox/Tanaka/Gulati); resting HR is a 7-day trailing average of morning readings, falling back to %HRmax when no reading is available in that window. "Below Z1" is rest between sets, not itself a training zone.
