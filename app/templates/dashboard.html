Recovery & fitness trends

Heart-rate and HRV metrics that reflect training load and recovery, not rhythm problems.

Weekly trend — last 12 weeks

Heart rate at rest, measured each session. Lower generally means better cardiovascular fitness or recovery; a rising trend can signal fatigue or illness.

Heart-rate recovery: how many BPM your heart rate drops in the 60 seconds after a session peak. Higher is better-conditioned; a chronic drop below ~12 BPM suggests poor recovery.

RMSSD: beat-to-beat heart-rate variability during the session. Higher generally reflects better parasympathetic (recovery) activity.

SDNN: overall heart-rate variability across the whole session, a broader HRV measure than RMSSD.

Cardiac drift: how much heart rate trends upward per minute at a steady effort — a sign of accumulating fatigue or heat/dehydration within a session.

Poincaré ratio (SD2/SD1): the balance between short-term and long-term beat-to-beat variability — a shape descriptor of heart-rhythm variability, not a rhythm-problem count.
